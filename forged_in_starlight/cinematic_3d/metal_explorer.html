Forge a Metal

The same waiting-point r-process model used in the merger sequence, made interactive. A neutron star merger's ejecta captures neutrons rapidly until it stalls at a shell closure (N = 50, 82, or 126). Once the free neutrons run out — roughly a second after merger — the nucleus beta-decays down to whichever stable element sits at that mass number. Set how far the capture chain runs and how many extra neutrons it grabs before freeze-out, and see which real element comes out the other end.
